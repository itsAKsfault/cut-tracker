    Breathing, the short version

    1 · Brace before every rep

    Take a normal breath into your belly and sides, then tighten your midsection as if someone's about to poke your stomach. About 30–50% effort, not a full clench. Ribs stay down, not flared.

    2 · Exhale on the hard part

    Breathe out as you push, pull, or lift. Breathe in as you return to the start. The exhale helps keep the brace.

    3 · Keep breathing during holds

    On planks and carries, take short, steady breaths behind the brace. Count breaths instead of seconds if it helps. If you can't breathe, the hold is too hard.

    4 · Don't hold your breath long

    Holding your breath through a whole rep spikes blood pressure. That matters more for anyone with heart or blood pressure issues. At these weights you never need to.
