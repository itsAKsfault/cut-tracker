Batch-Cook Guide

A meal system for people who would rather not cook. Two short cook sessions a week (Sunday and Wednesday) fill the fridge, and every meal after that is reheat-and-assemble in under 5 minutes. Built around chicken, with turkey, fish and beef for variety.

The calorie targets here are a placeholder, not your number.

1,800–1,900 kcal fits many men trying to lose weight while lifting estimate, but it depends on height, weight, age and current intake. The rule is the same for everyone: if your weekly average weight hasn't dropped in 2 weeks, cut about 150–200 kcal (one less rice portion or one less slice of bread a day).

Before you start

Three things to know

Calories do the work, not carbs

Cutting carbs works mainly because it cuts calories. The first-week drop of a few pounds is mostly water typical, varies. Don't go to zero carbs while lifting. The plan keeps a moderate amount, mostly as rice on workout days.

Your current foods aren't the enemy, the portions are

Frozen meals and good bread can stay. The problem is subs and drive-through runs adding up, and frozen meals being low in protein. The plan keeps both as tools instead of banning them.

No shakes means protein takes planning

Hitting 160+ g from food alone means about 6 oz of meat at lunch and dinner, plus protein at breakfast and a snack. That's exactly why the fridge stays stocked with cooked chicken.

The system

Two cook sessions a week

Sunday · about 45 min

  • Air fryer: 3 lb chicken breast or thighs, half lemon-pepper, half taco seasoning. 2 or 3 batches.
  • Rice cooker: 2 cups dry rice (makes about 6 cups cooked). If it has a steamer tray, steam broccoli on top at the same time.
  • Steamer or stove: 6–8 hard-boiled eggs for snacks, if you eat eggs.
  • No-cook: wash baby carrots, cucumbers, snap peas. Portion into containers.

Wednesday · about 30 min

  • Stove: 1.5 lb 93% lean ground turkey with a diced bell pepper and onion, taco seasoning. One pan, about 12 minutes.
  • Air fryer: 1.5 lb more chicken to carry you to Sunday.
  • Rice cooker: another 1–2 cups dry rice if you're out.
  • Cook fresh, not in batches: salmon and steak. Both take 10–15 minutes and taste worse reheated.
One tool worth buying: an instant-read meat thermometer.

Air fryers vary a lot, so cooking times are only a starting point. Safe minimum internal temperatures (USDA guidance): chicken and ground turkey 165°F, salmon 145°F, steak 145°F then rest 3 minutes. Cooked meat keeps about 3–4 days in the fridge, which is why there are two cook days.

Recipes, if you can call them that

Five things to cook

Air fryer chicken

  • Pat dry, light oil spray, season heavily.
  • Breasts: 380°F, about 18–22 min, flip halfway.
  • Thighs: 400°F, about 18–22 min. More forgiving if you overcook.
  • Done at 165°F. Cut thick breasts in half so they cook evenly.
  • Variation: soak in pickle juice 30 min first for pickle-brined chicken.

Turkey taco skillet

  • Brown 1.5 lb ground turkey in a pan on medium-high, about 7 min.
  • Add diced pepper and onion, cook 4 min.
  • Add taco seasoning and a splash of water, simmer 2 min.
  • Makes about 4–5 portions.

Air fryer salmon

  • Spray, salt, pepper, lemon pepper or Cajun.
  • 400°F, about 8–12 min depending on thickness. No flipping.
  • Done at 145°F, or when it flakes.

Stovetop steak

  • Salt the steak, heat a cast iron or heavy pan until very hot.
  • About 3–5 min per side for 1-inch sirloin.
  • Rest 3 min before cutting. Sirloin is leaner and cheaper than ribeye.

Rice cooker rice + steamed veg

  • Rinse rice, use the line markings or the manual's water ratio.
  • Frozen broccoli or green beans in the steamer tray, if yours has one. Zero chopping.
  • Portion rice into ½ or ¾ cup containers so you don't free-pour.

Flavor without calories

  • Hot sauce, salsa, mustard, lemon pepper, Cajun, taco seasoning, garlic powder, soy sauce, pickle juice, vinegar.
  • Watch: ranch, mayo, cheese sauce, oil poured straight from the bottle. One tablespoon of oil is about 120 kcal.

Grocery list

Protein

  • Chicken breast or thighs, 4–5 lb
  • 93% lean ground turkey, 1.5 lb
  • Salmon, 2 fillets (about 12 oz)
  • Sirloin steak, about 8 oz raw
  • Eggs, 18 (if you eat them)
  • Sliced turkey, 1 lb (if you eat deli meat)
  • Plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • Cheese sticks, shredded cheese (optional)

Carbs and produce

  • Rice, 2 lb bag (lasts 2+ weeks)
  • Frozen broccoli and green beans
  • Bagged salad greens
  • Bell peppers, onion, zucchini
  • Baby carrots, cucumbers, snap peas
  • Apples, berries
  • Good bread

Pantry

  • Olive oil spray
  • Lemon pepper, taco, Cajun, garlic powder, salt
  • Salsa, hot sauce, mustard, pickles
  • Light dressing or hummus
  • Small handful-size bag of nuts
  • Meal-prep containers
  • Instant-read thermometer
If a dog lives with you

Don't share seasoned leftovers. Onion and garlic, including powders and taco seasoning, are toxic to dogs. If you want to share, set aside a piece of chicken before seasoning and check with your vet about how much is OK.

Getting started

Two ways in

Just start

Do the Sunday cook this weekend and eat from the menu table. Weigh yourself every morning in the tracker and judge the weekly average. Adjust calories only after 2 weeks of data.

Measure first

Before changing anything, log what you actually eat for 7 days in the tracker, subs and takeout included. That gives your real starting intake, so the deficit is based on your numbers instead of an estimate.
